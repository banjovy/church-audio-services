# Live Captions & Audio — Viewer Guide

## What Is This?

Live captions and audio streaming are available during services. The words spoken from the stage appear as text on your phone or tablet a few seconds after they are said — similar to closed captions on TV. You can also listen to the live audio through your earphones or hearing device.

Please note: there is a short delay — some seconds — between what the speaker says and when the text appears on your screen. This is normal.

## How to View Captions on Your Phone

1. Make sure your phone is connected to the church WiFi.
2. Open your phone's camera and scan the QR code posted in the lobby, or type this address into your browser: **http://audio.local:8080**
3. Choose a display mode:
   - **Scrolling** — a running list of everything said, like a chat. You can scroll back to re-read something you missed. Works great on phones.
   - **TV Display** — large text, centered on screen. Best for tablets or if you prop your phone up.
   - **Current Only** — shows just the most recent phrase. Simple and distraction-free.

That's it. Captions will appear automatically. No app to install, no account to create.

## How to Listen to Live Audio

1. Make sure your phone is connected to the church WiFi.
2. Go to **http://audio.local:8080** and tap **Listen**.
3. Tap the play button and plug in your earphones or connect your hearing device via Bluetooth.

The audio stream has less than one second of delay. You can use it as a hearing assist — listen through your own earphones anywhere in the building.

## Tips

- Keep your phone screen on while viewing captions. Captions stop updating if the screen turns off.
- Audio streaming continues even if the screen is off (on most phones).
- If the text stops appearing, check that you're still on the church WiFi.
- If it says "Reconnecting..." just wait a moment — it will reconnect on its own.
- If it's unable to reconnect after about 10 minutes, it will stop trying and let you know. This is normal when the service ends and the system is no longer active. Just close the tab. Next time you're back, open the page again or refresh and it will connect right up.
- You can bookmark the page for quick access next time.
